Questions

Do I need a computer or a special printer?

No. Everything happens on your phone. Print the A4 PDF on any printer or at a print shop, then cut along the marks.

Can I import my staff or student list?

Yes. Import an Excel or CSV file, then add photos one by one or in bulk. You can export the list again any time.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. Use Backup to save a file you can restore on another phone.

Can I make government ID cards?

No. CardCraft is for your own organisation’s cards: staff, students, members, visitors and events.
