InvoiceAtlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

InvoiceAtlas ("the app") lets you create invoices, estimates, receipts and other business documents. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved only on your phone or in your browser. That includes your business profile, clients, items, documents, payments, expenses, logo and signature. We do not run a server that receives this data, and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, the data is deleted unless you made a backup.

Backups and files you export

When you export a PDF, CSV or backup file, the file is saved to your device or handed to an app you choose through the Share menu, such as email, WhatsApp or Drive. After that, the app you chose handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Sending documents

When you email or share a document, the app opens your own email or messaging app with the PDF attached. InvoiceAtlas does not send email itself, and it does not store the addresses you send to anywhere except on your device.

AI features

The "Use ChatGPT / own AI" feature does not send anything automatically. It shows a text request that you can copy into an AI service of your choice. You then paste the answer back into the app. Anything you paste into that service is covered by that service's privacy policy.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. Google tells the app whether your account has an active subscription so that Premium features can be unlocked.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only for Google Play purchases and to open links you tap. If you add a logo, signature or photo, the app reads only the image you pick.

Children

InvoiceAtlas is a business tool. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when advertising is added, we will update this page and the date above.
