CardCraft – ID Card Maker: Privacy Policy

Developer: Shadinota Tech, Khulna, Bangladesh · Effective date: 16 October 2026

In short: the app does not have accounts, does not send your cards, photos or people's details to us or anyone else. Everything you create stays on your phone unless you choose to export or share it. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device information as described below; Pro has no ads.

What the app stores

To make ID cards, you can type names, designations, ID numbers, blood groups, phone numbers, dates and other fields, and add photos, logos and signatures. All of this is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. We cannot see it.

Camera and photos

If you take or choose a photo, the app uses it only to place it on the card. Photos are resized and kept on your device. The app does not upload them.

Files you export

When you export a PDF, PNG images, a list (CSV) or a backup file, the file is created on your device. If you share it, it goes only where you send it, through your phone's share menu.

Purchases

CardCraft Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never receive them. The app only receives whether your Google account owns Pro, so it can unlock Pro features. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

Internet use

The app works offline. It connects to the internet to talk to Google Play for purchases and subscription checks and, in the free version, to load ads.

Advertising and analytics

The free version shows a small banner on some screens and, only if you choose, a short video ad that removes the watermark from one export. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and app performance and diagnostic data. Your cards, photos and people's details are never given to the ad network.

In regions where the law requires it (for example the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), and you can turn off personalised ads there. Buying Pro removes all ads. Google's own policy explains how it uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The app has no analytics or tracking tools of its own.

Children

The app is a tool for offices, schools and events. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Start over in the app, or uninstall the app, to delete everything stored by it. Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Responsible use

The app is for making office, school and event ID cards for people who agreed to it. Do not use it to copy government documents or to make cards for people without their consent.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do. Questions: shadinotatech@gmail.com
